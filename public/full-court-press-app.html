Straight talk about what this is.

This is a tool you run yourself — not an agency, not a scouting service, and not a database coaches browse. Nobody here contacts coaches for you, and no one can promise you exposure. What it does is make your outreach organized and consistent, which is what actually moves the needle. Everything saves privately on your own device.

If you're under 18, loop in a parent or guardian before you start emailing college programs — and check NCAA or NAIA contact rules for your sport and grad year, since there are periods when coaches can't respond.

Where your information lives

Everything you enter stays on this device. There's no account and no server holding your data, so there's nothing for anyone to breach remotely — but that also means it's protected by your phone or laptop lock, not by a password.

Two things follow from that: clearing your browser data erases your roster, camps, and film, and anyone who can unlock this device can open this app. Don't use a shared or school computer for anything you'd want kept private. Sign-in links and real accounts are coming with the hosted version.
