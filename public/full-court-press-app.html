Straight talk about what this is.

This is a tool you run yourself — not an agency, not a scouting service, and not a database coaches browse. Nobody here contacts coaches for you, and no one can promise you exposure. What it does is make your outreach organized and consistent, which is what actually moves the needle. Your roster, camps, and film stay in this browser unless you publish a profile — then that snapshot lives in your account, protected by your sign-in link, and the published page itself is public to anyone who has its link.

If you're under 18, loop in a parent or guardian before you start emailing college programs — and check NCAA or NAIA contact rules for your sport and grad year, since there are periods when coaches can't respond.
